# The Scarlet's Mistake || The Bull's Blade

A Formula 1 fanfiction following Hendryx Huntyr, Ferrari's golden prospect turned Red Bull Racing driver, as he challenges the expectations of the team that created him and the champion whose kingdom he was never supposed to threaten.

This repository hosts an HTML and CSS-based reading website featuring original storytelling, custom chapter layouts, and fictional motorsport media graphics designed to accompany the narrative.

## Synopsis

Ferrari spent years building  
Hendryx Huntyr for one purpose.

**To wear red.**

From a rain-soaked karting circuit  
in Belgium to the top step of Formula 2,  
every victory carried him closer to Maranello.

By nineteen, he is Ferrari's Golden Prospect.  
A champion shaped by their academy,  
adored by the Tifosi, and seemingly destined  
for the most famous seat in motorsports.

Then Ferrari chooses  
the Seven-time World Champion.

A living legend and a chance  
at history too tempting to ignore.

With two seats filled and neither  
belonging to Hendryx,  
he walks away...

**Red Bull Racing is waiting.**

They don't offer him promises or destiny.  
They offer him the most unforgiving  
opportunity on the grid:

A difficult car.  
A ruthless team environment.  
And a garage shared directly with Max Verstappen.

The Four-time World Champion has spent years turning  
Red Bull into his kingdom, dismantling teammates and  
rewriting the limits of what an elite driver can do.

Hendryx isn't expected to overthrow him.  
At first, surviving would be enough.  
But Hendryx has spent his entire career learning.

Learning cars.  
Reading rivals.  
Waiting for the exact moment to strike.

Now the prodigy Ferrari spent years creating belongs to  
their greatest enemy, carrying everything Maranello  
taught him into a machine that refuses to be tamed.

And as the 2025 season begins, Ferrari's decision stops  
looking like the end of one young driver's dream.

It starts looking like the beginning of their biggest mistake.

**Because Red Bull didn't steal Ferrari's future.**

**Ferrari left the blade lying on the table.  
Red Bull simply picked it up.**

## Project Structure

```text
scarlet-x-bull/
├── chapters/
│   └── chapter-XX-title.html
├── css/
│   ├── chapter.css
│   ├── global.css
│   └── index.css
├── images/
│   ├── chapter-XX/
│   └── poster-image/
├── js/
│   └── chapter.js
├── .gitignore
├── index.html
├── LICENSE
└── README.md
```

The `chapters/` directory contains individual HTML pages for each chapter, following a consistent naming convention.

The `css/` directory manages the website's typography, styling, and responsive layouts.

The `images/` directory contains chapter-specific illustrations, fictional motorsport media graphics, and promotional artwork.

The `js/` directory contains JavaScript functionality for chapter navigation, reading progress, and interactive elements.

Chapter filenames and image directories follow a consistent numbering system, allowing new chapters to be added without changing the overall project structure.

Locally stored font files are excluded from version control due to third-party licensing restrictions.
